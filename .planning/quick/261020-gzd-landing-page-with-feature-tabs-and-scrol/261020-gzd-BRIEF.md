## Architecture (locked)

- Stay a plain-DOM, single static Vite app, offline, no router library, no new dependencies, do not touch `src/core/`. - The existing tests may assert on `index.html` structure; update them narrowly and keep their intent. Add DOM-free Vitest for the pure parts (route parsing, tab model + keyboard model, scroll-progress to step mapping, formatting).
