# Phase 4 Plan 04: Suspect drawer, Markdown postmortem copy, and the trust footer

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 4 goal (verbatim): "Make the analysis useful in the browser through one-click case studies, an evidence-first verdict, linked timeline investigation, and recoverable accessible states."

## Planning decisions honored

Decision IDs are the planner-assigned IDs from Plan 04-01.

- D-02: changed files and line ranges come from `stream.commits`; signals, evidence and scores from `toAnalysisResult(stream).suspects`.
- D-05: drawer, footer and fallback textarea receive data only through `textContent`, `setAttribute` and the textarea `value` property.
- D-09: no slide animation; the drawer simply opens.
- D-10: drawer math uses 3 decimals with "rounded for display"; the copied Markdown keeps full precision.
- D-14 (UI-SPEC Data contract and Suspect drawer): the history export has changed line ranges and +/- counts, not diff text; the drawer says so and never fabricates hunks.
- D-15 (UI-SPEC Copy flow): source is `serializeMarkdown(toAnalysisResult(stream))` computed on demand; primary path `navigator.clipboard.writeText`; any failure reveals the selected fallback textarea; no auto-dismiss timer; the button label never changes.

Planner discretion: the copy control is also rendered under the state panel for no-incident, insufficient-data and no-eligible-commits results ("Markdown copy still available"); `formatInteger` is added to format.ts for the limits line ('10,000 events') without any locale API.

Edge-coverage source: spec-less fallback; edge predicates come from the UI-SPEC "UI Considerations" table and the ROADMAP Phase 4 success criteria.

<objective>
Let the reader inspect why a suspect ranks where it does and take the result away: a suspect drawer with one plain-language explanation per ranking signal and the real line-range evidence (UX-05), reachable from the verdict, the lineup and the timeline markers (UX-02 actions); a deterministic Markdown postmortem copy with visible success or fallback feedback (UX-07); and a trust footer that keeps warnings, diagnostics, assumptions and the UTC note visible (UX-08), all keyboard-operable at both widths (UX-09).

Purpose: completes the evidence chain from verdict to verifiable detail and a shareable, honest write-up.
Output: drawer model and renderer, copy module and feedback reducer, trust footer, and their tests.
</objective>
