# Phase 4 Plan 06: The suspect drawer

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 4 goal (verbatim): "Make the analysis useful in the browser through one-click case studies, an evidence-first verdict, linked timeline investigation, and recoverable accessible states."

## Planning decisions honored

Decision IDs come from the register in Plan 04-01 plus D-14 added here.

- D-02: changed files and line ranges come from `stream.commits`; signals, evidence and scores from `toAnalysisResult(stream).suspects`.
- D-05: drawer content is text only via `src/ui/dom.ts`.
- D-09: no slide animation; the drawer simply opens.
- D-10: drawer arithmetic uses 3 decimals with "rounded for display".
- D-14 (UI-SPEC Data contract and Suspect drawer): the history export has changed line ranges and +/- counts, not diff text; the drawer says so and never fabricates hunks.

Edge-coverage source: spec-less fallback; edge predicates come from the UI-SPEC "UI Considerations" table and the ROADMAP Phase 4 success criteria.

Revision note (checker revision 1): this is Tasks 1-2 of the former Plan 04-04; the copy flow and trust footer (its former Task 3) are Plan 04-07.

<objective>
Let the reader inspect why a suspect ranks where it does: a drawer with one plain-language explanation per ranking signal and the real changed-line evidence (UX-05), reachable from the verdict, the lineup and the timeline markers (UX-02 actions), keyboard-operable at both widths (UX-09).

Purpose: completes the evidence chain from verdict to verifiable detail.
Output: drawer model and renderer, opener wiring in the verdict, lineup and timeline, and tests.
</objective>
