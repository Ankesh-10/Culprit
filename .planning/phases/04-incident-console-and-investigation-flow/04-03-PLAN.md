# Phase 4 Plan 03: Evidence-first verdict card and the suspect lineup

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 4 goal (verbatim): "Make the analysis useful in the browser through one-click case studies, an evidence-first verdict, linked timeline investigation, and recoverable accessible states."

## Planning decisions honored

Decision IDs come from the register in Plan 04-01 plus two added here.

- D-01: plain DOM, native CSS, no new dependency.
- D-02: suspects, confidence, ranking and interpretation come from `toAnalysisResult(stream)`.
- D-05: all data strings via `textContent` / `setAttribute` through `src/ui/dom.ts`.
- D-07: four type sizes, two weights, token colours; accent only on meter fills and pressed toggles; the leading-suspect aqua only on the #1 rank diamond.
- D-10 (UI-SPEC Typography and Verdict card): scores show 2 decimals on cards with the visible caption "rounded for display"; the Markdown keeps full precision (Plan 04-07).
- D-11 (UI-SPEC Copywriting voice): calm, evidence-first copy; never "culprit found", "caused", "root cause", "broke production" or percentages.

Edge-coverage source: spec-less fallback; edge predicates come from the UI-SPEC "UI Considerations" table and the ROADMAP Phase 4 success criteria.

Sequencing note: the verdict's and lineup's "Inspect evidence" and "See all evidence" controls arrive with the drawer in Plan 04-06, and "Copy Markdown postmortem" with the copy flow in Plan 04-07, so no inert button ships in this plan.

Revision note (checker revision 1): this is the former Plan 04-02, renumbered after the split of the former Plan 04-01; its scope is unchanged.

<objective>
Turn the headline into the full evidence-first verdict card (UX-02) and add the "Suspect lineup" top-three shortlist with an explicit "Commits considered" list, so a reader sees within ten seconds who to check first, why, how strongly, and what was excluded, with uncertainty in words and shapes, not colour alone (UX-08), at laptop and phone widths (UX-09).

Purpose: the product's core value on screen, a deterministic, evidence-backed shortlist that never implies proof.
Output: `src/ui/format.ts`, the extended verdict view and renderer, `src/ui/shortlist.ts`, styles, and the format and view-model test suites.
</objective>
