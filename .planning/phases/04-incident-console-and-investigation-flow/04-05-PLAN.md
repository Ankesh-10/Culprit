# Phase 4 Plan 05: Error signatures that filter the investigation

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 4 goal (verbatim): "Make the analysis useful in the browser through one-click case studies, an evidence-first verdict, linked timeline investigation, and recoverable accessible states."

## Planning decisions honored

Decision IDs come from the register in Plan 04-01 and Plan 04-04: D-02 (signature counts and event ids from the kept stream), D-05 (text-only DOM and SVG), D-12 (emphasis form adds no colours: total in context gray, selection in the errors blue; never colour alone), D-13 (filters live in one row above the chart; chart, readout, log panel and table twin re-render against the same filter).

Planner discretion (recorded for the checker):

- Log panel scope: opened from a signature toggle (or, in Plan 04-06, from the drawer) it shows all events of that signature; opened from the slider, readout or a table-twin row it shows the selected interval's events intersected with the active filter. The header names the scope.
- Filtered readout reads `{filtered} of {total} errors`.
- Clearing the filter closes a signature-scope log panel and leaves a bucket-scope panel open.

Edge-coverage source: spec-less fallback; edge predicates come from the UI-SPEC "UI Considerations" table and the ROADMAP Phase 4 success criteria.

Revision note (checker revision 1): this is the former Plan 04-03 Task 3, split out into its own plan and divided into a tracer plus a completion task.

<objective>
Let the reader pick an error signature and see only its errors across the chart, readout, log lines and data table (UX-06), reaching the matching log lines without hover (UX-04), keyboard-operable at both widths (UX-09).

Purpose: connects the "what failed" view (signatures) to the "when" view (timeline) so a person can confirm which error the suspect explains.
Output: `src/ui/signatures.ts`, the signature filter in the investigation model, the emphasis form in the timeline, and tests.
</objective>
