# Phase 4 Plan 02: Evidence-first verdict card and the suspect lineup

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 4 goal (verbatim): "Make the analysis useful in the browser through one-click case studies, an evidence-first verdict, linked timeline investigation, and recoverable accessible states."

## Planning decisions honored

Decision IDs are the planner-assigned IDs from Plan 04-01 (no CONTEXT.md exists; the approved UI-SPEC is the design context).

- D-01: plain DOM, native CSS, no new dependency.
- D-02: suspects, confidence, ranking and interpretation come from `toAnalysisResult(stream)`; the stream is kept for regions that need dropped fields.
- D-05: all data strings via `textContent` / `setAttribute` through `src/ui/dom.ts`.
- D-07: four type sizes, two weights, token colours; the accent is used only for meter fills and pressed toggles here; the leading-suspect aqua appears only on the #1 rank diamond.
- D-10 (UI-SPEC Typography and Verdict card): scores show 2 decimals on cards with the visible caption "rounded for display"; the Markdown keeps full precision (Plan 04-04).
- D-11 (UI-SPEC Copywriting voice): calm, evidence-first copy; never "culprit found", "caused", "root cause", "broke production" or percentages.

Edge-coverage source: spec-less fallback; edge predicates come from the UI-SPEC "UI Considerations" table and the ROADMAP Phase 4 success criteria.

Sequencing note: the verdict's "Inspect evidence", "See all evidence" and "Copy Markdown postmortem" controls and the lineup's "Inspect evidence" buttons are added in Plan 04-04 together with the drawer and copy flow they operate, so no inert button ships in this plan.

<objective>
Turn the Plan 04-01 headline into the full evidence-first verdict card (UX-02) and add the "Suspect lineup" top-three shortlist with an explicit "Commits considered" list, so a reader sees in under ten seconds who to check first, why, how strongly, and what was excluded, with uncertainty stated in words and shapes, not colour alone (UX-08), at laptop and phone widths (UX-09).

Purpose: this is the product's core value on screen: a deterministic, evidence-backed shortlist that never implies proof.
Output: `src/ui/format.ts`, extended `src/ui/view-model.ts` and `src/ui/verdict.ts`, new `src/ui/shortlist.ts`, styles, and the format and view-model test suites.
</objective>
